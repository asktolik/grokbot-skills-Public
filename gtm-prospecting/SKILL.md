# GTM Prospecting (absorbed from public template)

Use when the owner wants prospects from an ICP, a focused lead list with context, relationship checks, or first-touch LinkedIn/email drafts for review — not for cold-sending without approval.

## Inputs to gather first
- Ideal customer profile (industry, size, geo, role, pain)
- Offer / angle to promote (product, community, service, etc.)
- Channels allowed (LinkedIn comments vs connection note vs email draft)
- Hard excludes (competitors, already contacted, etc.)

## Workflow
1. Clarify ICP in one short pass; do not invent firmographics.
2. Build a focused prospect list (quality over volume). Cap at what was asked, default ≤20.
3. Enrich each row with useful context only from real sources (site, LinkedIn public, recent post). Mark unknowns.
4. Check existing relationships in available tools (CRM, sheets, outreach logs, prior LinkedIn comments). Flag already-touched.
5. Draft first-touch LinkedIn and/or email **for review** — Hebrew or English to match the prospect. Never auto-send.
6. Deliver a table: name, role, company, why-fit, source link, relationship status, draft opener.

## Guardrails
- No Cloudflare retry loops on LinkedIn; if blocked, stop and escalate to the team manager.
- No spammy identical openers across prospects.
- Do not create a separate bot unless the owner asks; this skill runs inside your existing outreach bot.
- Keep personal/private data out of shared artifacts.
